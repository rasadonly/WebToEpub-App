How to Convert Web Novels to EPUB

Follow these simple steps to download any web novel as a beautifully formatted EPUB file, ready for your Kindle, Kobo, or any e-reader.

Start Converting →

Step 1: Find Your Novel

Go to any supported novel site and copy the table-of-contents URL.

Step 2: Paste & Fetch

Paste the URL into LinkToEpub and click Fetch Chapters.

Step 3: Download EPUB

Select your chapters, click Generate, and download your EPUB.
